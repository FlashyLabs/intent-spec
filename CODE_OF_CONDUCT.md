# Code of conduct

Be direct, be specific, and argue about documents rather than people. A
specification is improved by somebody saying plainly that a rule is wrong and
showing the document that breaks it; it is not improved by anyone being made
unwelcome for saying so.

Not acceptable, anywhere this project is discussed: harassment, personal
attacks, discriminatory remarks, publishing somebody's private information, or
sustained disruption of a discussion.

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline any contribution or comment that
breaks this, and may remove a contributor who keeps breaking it. They will say
why.

To report a problem, email **security@flashylabs** (the same private channel
as `SECURITY.md`; the address is to be confirmed before launch) with "conduct"
in the subject. Reports are read by a maintainer who was not involved, where
one exists, and are kept private.
